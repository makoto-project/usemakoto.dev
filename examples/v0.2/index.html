v0.2 examples and clearly labeled historical scenarios

Data provenance fails in familiar, expensive ways.

Makoto is useful when data leaves its source and somebody later needs to prove what happened. These examples connect the protocol to incident response, scientific reproduction, releases, AI datasets, policy enforcement, and handoffs between organizations.

Status matters: the producer-to-receiver handoff is the current runnable v0.2 proof. The six scenario demos and SQuAD DBOM are preserved v0.1 design material. They explain the problem well, but their wire format is not v0.2-compatible.

v0.2 lesson

Unknown is better than invented

A provenance record should distinguish verified bytes and signed claims from reconstructed narrative. Makoto cannot retroactively create evidence that the source pipeline never emitted.
